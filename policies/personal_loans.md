# Personal loans: illustrative policy

This sample policy is illustrative and is not a bank offer, a credit decision, financial advice, or a statement of any real institution's policy.

## Illustrative eligibility checks

For this application, a customer must report monthly salary of at least ₹25,000. Existing monthly EMI plus the proposed personal-loan EMI must be no more than 50% of reported monthly salary. Eligibility is an illustrative calculation from values entered by the customer. A result marked eligible is not approval and does not guarantee an actual loan.

## EMI estimate

The calculator estimates a reducing-balance monthly instalment from loan principal, annual interest rate, and tenure. The estimate excludes fees, insurance, taxes, and other charges. Actual lender quotations may use different rates, terms, and fees.

## Application handling

An eligible application explicitly confirmed by the customer is stored in a local CSV file and assigned a reference number with status “Approved - Simulated.” This is only an application simulation; no bank receives the application, no credit bureau is checked, and no money is disbursed. Review the displayed amount, rate, tenure, and EMI and explicitly confirm before submitting.
